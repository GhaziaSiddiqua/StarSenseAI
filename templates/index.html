How it works

StarSense AI uses a trained classification pipeline to map stellar measurements to one of six categories represented in its dataset.

The pipeline preprocesses numeric and categorical inputs before making a prediction. The website sends your measurements to a FastAPI backend, which returns the predicted category.

The prediction is a model output, not a confirmed astronomical diagnosis.
