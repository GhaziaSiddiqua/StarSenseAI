Six worlds of starlight

The six target categories represented in the training dataset.

CLASS 00 ●

Brown Dwarf

A substellar object that does not sustain ordinary hydrogen fusion.

CLASS 01 ✦

Red Dwarf

A small, relatively cool star with a long potential lifetime.

CLASS 02 ✧

White Dwarf

A compact stellar remnant left after a suitable star evolves.

CLASS 03 ✹

Main Sequence

The stage when a star fuses hydrogen into helium in its core.

CLASS 04 ✷

Supergiant

An exceptionally large and luminous star in an advanced stage.

CLASS 05 ✺

Hypergiant

An extremely luminous, massive star that can lose mass rapidly.

Inside the observatory / 03

The science behind the screen

A look at the educational machine learning pipeline.

From measurements to prediction

StarSense AI takes four numeric measurements and two categorical features. The saved preprocessing and classification pipeline transforms the inputs and predicts one of the six categories represented in the dataset.

The frontend sends JSON to the FastAPI /predict endpoint and displays the category returned by the model.

This is an educational demonstration, not a validated tool for professional astronomical classification.
